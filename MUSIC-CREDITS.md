**Changes to recordings:** The files inside `public/music` are compressed
re-encodings of the composer’s originals (80 kbps MP3, 44.1 kHz stereo), to
limit initial game-download size. No musical remix, rearrangement, or additions.
Playback is looped and faded between maps. The artist does not endorse or
sponsor Vanguard Ops.

To refresh the files, run the **Vendor licensed background music** workflow
or execute `bash scripts/vendor-licensed-music.sh` in an environment with
`curl`, `ffmpeg`, and `ffprobe`, then commit `public/music/*.mp3`.

The site owner is responsible for preserving these credits in redistributions.
