## Rendering contract

- Gold Standard is plated gold with visible dark bronze/black hardware, model-specific inserts and readable mechanical detailing in first- and third-person.
- Animated Prestige camos coat the full model and have `Prestige` display rarity; original IDs and ownership are unchanged.
- The carbon-fiber weave and physically based wear are aesthetic direction, not dedicated texture/normal-map assets in the current renderer.
- Prestige cosmetics require all seven ordinary challenges; a Wonder token additionally requires rank 5/5 Weapon Mastery.
